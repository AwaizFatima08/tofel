Take the TOEFL before the TOEFL

Full-length, timed simulations of the new TOEFL iBT, the format in use since January 2026: adaptive Reading and Listening, Writing and Speaking, scored on the 1-6 band scale with a CEFR level and a comparable 0-120 total. Then every answer explained.

Coming soon on Google Play

20 full-length mock tests

The real pattern

Reading (50 items, about 30 min), Listening (47 items, about 29 min), Writing (12 items, 23 min) and Speaking (11 items, 8 min), each part timed and introduced as on test day.

Adaptive like the real test

Your first Reading and Listening module decides whether the second is easier or harder, and only the harder module opens bands 5 and 6.

Bands and explanations

Estimated 1-6 bands per section and overall, the CEFR level and a 0-120 comparison, why each answer is right, transcripts of every recording, and model answers for every writing and speaking task.

What you get

  • All 13 task types. Complete the Words, Read in Daily Life, Read an Academic Passage, Listen and Choose a Response, conversations, announcements, academic talks, Build a Sentence, Write an Email, Academic Discussion, Listen and Repeat and Take an Interview.
  • Real listening. Every recording is spoken aloud by your phone's English voices and plays once in a mock, as on the test; in practice you can replay it and read the transcript.
  • Speaking with your voice. Answer into the microphone with the real time limits, play your answer back and compare it with a model answer.
  • Lessons for every task. What is on screen, a step-by-step method, worked examples, traps and templates for the email and discussion posts.
  • Separate practice. Practice items for each task with instant answers, so practice never spoils a mock.
  • Guidance, tips and study plans. The 2026 format and scoring, test day and Home Edition, time management, note-taking, common mistakes, and 4 and 8-week plans.
  • Vocabulary flashcards. Academic words, phrasal verbs and idioms with meanings, examples and synonyms.
  • Private and offline. No account. Your answers and voice recordings stay on your phone.
