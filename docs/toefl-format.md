# TOEFL iBT format (research notes, 4 Nov 2026)

Sources: ets.org test content page, score page, and the ETS "TOEFL iBT Test: 2026 Update — Test Blueprint and
Specifications Document" (PDF linked from ets.org). Third-party prep sites were used only for task-level detail
that ETS does not publish (marked *).

| Section | Tasks | Items | Time | Design |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reading | Complete the Words, Read in Daily Life, Read an Academic Passage | 50 (35 raw points; some unscored) | ~30 min (router 18-21, module 9) | two-stage adaptive |
| Listening | Listen and Choose a Response, Conversation, Announcement, Academic Talk | 47 | ~29 min (router 18, lower 7 / upper 11) | two-stage adaptive |
| Writing | Build a Sentence ×10, Write an Email, Write for an Academic Discussion | 12 (20 raw points) | 23 min | linear |
| Speaking | Listen and Repeat ×7, Take an Interview ×4 | 11 (55 raw points) | 8 min | linear |

- Total test time 1 h 23 min to 1 h 29 min; allow about 2 hours with directions. Notes allowed. No scheduled break*.
- Reading texts 15-200 words; Complete the Words: one paragraph, 10 words with the second half missing*. Daily Life:
  2- and 3-item sets. Academic passage ~200 words, ~5 items*.
- Listening audio plays once; brief utterances up to 250-word monologues; AI-generated and human voices with
  US/Canadian, British and Australian accents.
- Scoring: Reading/Listening 1 point per item; Build a Sentence 1 point; Email and Discussion 0-5; each Listen and
  Repeat and Interview item 0-5. Four section bands 1-6 in half bands; overall = mean rounded to the nearest half band;
  comparable 0-120 total (midpoint of the range) for two years after 21 Jan 2026.
- Old-scale concordance (total 0-120 → band): 114+ 6, 107+ 5.5, 95+ 5, 86+ 4.5, 72+ 4, 58+ 3.5, 44+ 3, 34+ 2.5,
  24+ 2, 12+ 1.5, else 1.
- Timing per writing task*: Build a Sentence ~6 min, Email 7 min, Discussion 10 min; Interview answers 45 s each*.

## How the app maps this
- Reading router: 2 Complete the Words + 1 short and 1 long Daily Life + 2 academic passages (35 points, 20 min);
  upper: 1 hard Complete the Words + 1 hard passage; lower: 1 easy Complete the Words + 2 Daily Life (15 points, 9 min).
- Listening router: 10 Choose a Response, 3 conversations, 2 announcements, 3 talks (32 items, 18 min); upper: 5 + 2
  + 1 + 1 talk (15 items, 11 min); lower: 7 + 2 + 2 (15 items, 7 min).
- Upper module when the router score reaches 60 %. Lower module caps the section band at 4.5.
